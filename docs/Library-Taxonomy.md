# Treasury of Truth

## Library Taxonomy

### Purpose

The Library Taxonomy establishes the canonical organization of knowledge within the Treasury of Truth.

Its purpose is to preserve, organize, relate, and present biblical truth in a consistent manner so that every resource may be accurately classified, easily discovered, and meaningfully connected to the whole counsel of God.

The taxonomy is designed to remain stable over time. While new subjects and resources will continually be added, the underlying structure shall change only when necessary to improve clarity, faithfulness, and stewardship.

## Core Principles

The Treasury of Truth Taxonomy shall:

- Be Scripture-centered.
- Organize knowledge according to the message of the Bible rather than merely academic disciplines.
- Preserve doctrinal consistency.
- Encourage readers to return continually to the biblical text.
- Support long-term stewardship of biblical resources.
- Provide stable classifications for future generations.
