# Treasury of Truth

## Canonical Knowledge Model

The Treasury of Truth preserves, organizes, and relates the following kinds of biblical knowledge.

## Resources

- Sermons
- Bible Studies
- Articles
- Books
- Devotionals
- Missionary Training
- Leadership Lessons
- Conference Notes
- Study Guides

## Scripture

The Holy Scriptures are the primary authority and organizing principle of the Treasury of Truth.

Resources may be connected to:

- Books
- Chapters
- Verses
- Passages
- Parallel Passages
- Cross References

## Canonical Subjects

- Biblical Doctrine
- Christian Living
- Ministry
- Missions
- Biblical History
- Prophecy
- Apologetics

## People

- Biblical People
- Historical People
- Contemporary Authors

## Places

- Biblical Places
- Historical Locations

## Events

- Biblical Events
- Historical Events

## Series

Series are ordered groups of related resources developed around a common purpose, biblical passage, doctrine, or teaching objective.

## Collections

Collections are curated groups of resources preserved together because of a shared purpose, history, audience, ministry setting, or subject.

## Annotations

Annotations provide concise supporting information that materially clarifies a resource or biblical passage.

They may include:

- Hidden Jewels
- Historical Notes
- Cultural Notes
- Geographical Notes
- Hebrew Insights
- Greek Insights

Hebrew and Greek insights shall be used only when the original text contributes a meaningful distinction, emphasis, or range of meaning not fully apparent in the cited Bible translation.

## Guiding Principle

Every element of the Canonical Knowledge Model exists to help readers understand, discover, and faithfully interpret the inspired Scriptures.

The organization of knowledge shall always serve the authority of the biblical text rather than replace or overshadow it.
